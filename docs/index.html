Termadoro

Termadoro waits between the seconds, and your focus calls it back. A focus timer for your terminal.

Twenty-five minutes of focus on one thing. Five of rest, so your light can settle. After four, a longer rest. Each interval you keep returns one of its memories.

It lives in the terminal, on Macs with Apple Silicon. Install it with Homebrew or the install script below, then run termadoro. Nothing leaves your machine.
